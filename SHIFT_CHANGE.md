## During work

Include completed work, changed docs, decisions, blockers with cause/next attempt/workaround/user input, and likely next priorities.

Report per-system freshness and sync outcome. If a target is missing/mismatched, stop that write; no substitute report. If one destination is unavailable, continue independently authorized unaffected work while leaving the combined sync incomplete.
